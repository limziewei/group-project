1. Introduction
The Restaurant Management System is a web-based platform designed to handle daily restaurant operations. It has three main interfaces:
Customer View – For guests to browse the menu and place orders.


Admin / Counter View – For staff to manage menu items, check revenue, and monitor inventory.


Kitchen View – For kitchen staff to receive and fulfill customer orders.

3. Customer View
Purpose: Allow customers to browse, select, and order food.
Step 3.1: Browse the Menu
Open the Customer Page.


View all available menu items with their:
Name, Description, and Price.

Step 3.2: Add Items to Cart
Enter the Quantity for the chosen item.


(Optional) Enter Remarks such as “No onions”.


Click Add to Cart.


The selected item is added to the customer’s shopping cart.


Step 3.3: Review Cart & Checkout
Click View Cart.


Review the order details in a table format:


Item | Price | Quantity | Remark | Line Total


Confirm the Total Price shown at the bottom.


Enter the Table Number (required for dine-in).


Choose an action:


Back to Menu – to add more items.


Proceed to Checkout – to finalize order.


Once confirmed, the order is sent directly to the Kitchen Dashboard.




4. Kitchen Staff Dashboard
Purpose: Enable kitchen staff to see, prepare, and complete customer orders.
Step 4.1: Read an Order Ticket
Each new order appears as a ticket card.


Information displayed:


Order ID – unique number.


Table Number – dining table (or blank for takeaway).


Total Price – full order cost.


Order Items – list of dishes with details:
Step 4.2: Process an Order
Start preparing items as soon as a new ticket appears.


Once the order is complete and delivered:


Click the Delete button on the ticket.


This removes the order from the queue and marks it as completed.
